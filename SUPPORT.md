# Report a problem

Found a bug, or something in Inkwell not working the way you expect? Email **robert@nightfallautomotive.co.uk**.

[Send an email](mailto:robert@nightfallautomotive.co.uk?subject=Inkwell%20problem)

To help get it fixed quickly, please include:

- What you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- How you run Inkwell: the Flatpak, `install.sh`, or `python3 server.py`.
- Your Linux distribution and desktop, for example Fedora 44 with GNOME.
- A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Please don't send your notes or your password.
